## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an **end-to-end Swiggy data analysis using Microsoft Excel**.

The objective is to transform a large food-delivery dataset into meaningful **business insights and an interactive dashboard** covering restaurant performance, food categories, pricing, ratings, cities, states, and time-based trends.

The project demonstrates practical **Data Analyst skills in Excel**, including data exploration, Pivot Tables, data aggregation, KPI creation, visualization, and business insight generation.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The analysis aims to answer questions such as:

* Which cities have the highest number of food listings?
* Which restaurants have the largest presence?
* What are the most popular food categories?
* How are food prices distributed?
* Which food types dominate the dataset?
* Which cities/restaurants have the highest ratings?
* How does activity change month-to-month and quarter-to-quarter?
* What business opportunities can be identified from the data?

---

## 📁 Dataset

The dataset contains:

* **197,430 records**
* **14 attributes**
* Data covering **January 2025 – August 2025**
* **28 states**

# 💡 Key Insights

### 📌 1. Large Dataset

The dataset contains **197,430 records**, providing a substantial base for exploring food delivery trends.

### 📌 2. Geographic Concentration

Some cities contribute significantly more records than others, indicating stronger restaurant/menu presence in those markets.

### 📌 3. Pricing Pattern

The average listed food price is approximately **₹269**, while the median is approximately **₹229**.

This indicates that relatively expensive items influence the overall average price.

### 📌 4. Strong Rating Profile

The overall average rating is approximately **4.34/5**, indicating generally positive customer feedback across the available records.

### 📌 5. Food-Type Distribution

The analysis identifies the dominant food type and allows comparison between different food segments.

### 📌 6. Category Opportunities

Certain food categories have substantially higher listing volumes, making them potential focus areas for promotions and restaurant partnerships.

### 📌 7. Restaurant Performance

Restaurant-level analysis helps identify businesses with strong menu presence and consistently high customer ratings.

### 📌 8. Time-Based Trends

Monthly, weekly, and quarterly analysis highlights fluctuations in food listing activity and helps identify periods of higher activity.
